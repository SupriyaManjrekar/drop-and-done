Drop a pile of bills. Get a claim that's ready to submit.

Drop & Done is an AI-powered reimbursement assistant. You drop every bill from a trip into one chat: hotel invoices, e-tickets, cab receipts, a faded café bill, a handwritten auto slip. It reads them, sorts them into trips, checks each one against policy, asks at most three questions, and hands your manager a claim that explains itself.

The AI reads. Rules decide.

An AI that decides your money can't explain itself. So the work is split, and every decision on the claim can be traced.

What the AI does

Reads each bill into strict, structured fields: vendor, date, amounts, tax numbers, line items. Every field gets a confidence score, and the AI reports any sign of editing or fabrication. Nothing else.

What the rules do

Duplicates, policy caps, alcohol, GST credit, date windows, arithmetic checks, trip grouping and the questions to ask. All of it is plain code that cites its policy clause.
