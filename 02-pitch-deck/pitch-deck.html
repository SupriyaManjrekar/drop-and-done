Who feels the pain

👤 Employee

  • Hunts for bills in email, WhatsApp and their wallet
  • Types every field by hand into a form
  • Has to guess the policy rules
  • Finds out about a rejection days later

✅ Approver

  • Gets 20 claim lines with no context
  • Either rubber-stamps them or digs through each one
  • Has no signal for what's risky

Feels: "I can't really check this"

  • Checks 100% of claims by hand
  • Chases missing GSTINs and invoices
  • Catches fraud only by sampling
  • Has no data on policy leakage

Feels: "drowning in rework"

Design principles

  • Zero forms. The chat is the whole interface.
  • Batch first. Handle the whole pile, not one receipt at a time.
  • Ask by exception. Only gaps that block submission; tap to answer.
  • Explain every flag. Say what's wrong, why, and how to fix it.
  • Always show status. ✅ complete · ⚠️ needs info · 🚩 questionable

North-star UX metric

Time-to-ready-to-submit
Target under 2 min for a 10-receipt trip, against about 20 min today.

Drop & Done05
AI intervention

Where AI does the work, and where rules keep it honest

1
Read every documentClaude vision turns photos, PDFs and screenshots into strict JSON: vendor, date, amount, tax, GSTIN, city and payment mode, with a confidence score per field.
2
Validate deterministicallyChecks that items + tax = total, the GSTIN checksum, that dates make sense, and converts foreign currency. These are rules, not guesses.
3
OrganiseGroups expenses into trips or events by date window, city and travel bookings.
4
Check policyRules-as-code by grade and city tier. The LLM handles fuzzy cases and writes the explanation.
5
Protect integrityDuplicates at 3 levels (image hash, same transaction, across employees), signals of AI-generated receipts, and anomalies against peers.
6
Recover GSTFlags missing company GSTIN while it can still be fixed, and tags eligible input credit.
7
Ask only what's missingLow-confidence or missing fields become at most 3 batched, tap-to-answer questions.
8
Prepare & routeBuilds the submission-ready claim, gives approvers a one-line summary, and sends finance a risk-ranked audit queue.

Human in the loop by design: AI prepares and flags, people approve payouts. Every AI decision is logged with the image region and policy clause it relied on.

Drop & Done07
The backend that makes it reliable

A claim is a state machine, not a form

Each extracted field carries evidence

"amount":  { "value": 4200,
             "confidence": 0.97,
             "source": "bottom-right total" },
"gstin":   { "value": null,
             "confidence": 0.0,
             "status": "MISSING" },
"category":{ "value": "Meals",
             "confidence": 0.88 }

Confidence ≥ 0.85 is auto-filled. Below that, or missing, it becomes a question. Anything contradictory is flagged.

Line states

COMPLETE NEEDS_INFO QUESTIONABLE EXCLUDED

Claim lifecycle

DRAFT → READY → SUBMITTED → APPROVED → PAID

A claim is READY only when every line is COMPLETE or EXCLUDED.

Category checklists

🏨 Hotel: invoice, stay dates, GSTIN, city tier
🍽 Meal > cap: attendees + purpose
🚕 Cab: from/to or trip link
✈️ Flight: boarding pass or e-ticket
